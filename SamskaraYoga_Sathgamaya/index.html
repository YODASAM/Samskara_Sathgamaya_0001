A quiet confession

You've already tried to handle your stress alone. It didn't fully hold.

I know this struggle personally — I carried my own stress for years, trying to meditate with no real guidance, no structure, and no one to turn to when it wasn't working. What finally released that stress for me wasn't a new technique. It was a structured, guided path.

No real structure

Trying a bit of this and a bit of that, with no clear order or method, keeps you starting over again and again instead of actually progressing.

Consistency dies alone

Day one is full of intention. By day four, the mat stays rolled up. Without someone expecting you to show up, the practice quietly slips off the calendar — not from a lack of will, but a lack of structure.

Your doubts go unanswered

"Am I doing this right?" "Why do I feel this way?" "Is this even working?" Alone, these questions pile up quietly until the whole practice starts to feel unclear, and unclear things get abandoned.

The Sathgamaya framework

Three live sessions. One teacher, guiding you all the way.

Every session is live over video call, so every participant gets real, personal attention. Here is exactly what happens each session.

01

Loosen — Upa Yoga

Simple joint and energy-activating movements to release the physical stiffness that makes sitting still feel impossible, guided live so you move with the group in real time.

02

Sit — Sadhguru-Guided Meditation

A Sadhguru-guided meditation practice, done together, live — the same structured method that shifted things for me.

03

Ask — Open Q&A

Ten to fifteen minutes where nothing is off-limits — technique, experience, or doubt. Ask me directly, and get an answer live.

→

Day 3 — a special Master Call

On the final session, I'll close with a short Master Call — capped to a small batch — for those who feel the shift and want to go further, a glimpse of what's possible for the ones ready to continue this journey with me.
