The practice

Not another workout.
A different quality of attention.

These sessions are designed around simplicity: a clear space, precise instruction, conscious movement and time to become still. You do not need to be flexible or experienced to begin.

01

Build steadiness

Move with awareness and establish a more stable relationship with your body and breath.

02

Create space

Step out of the noise of the day and give yourself an undistracted period of practice.

03

Carry it with you

Leave with a clearer sense of how to bring attention, balance and ease into ordinary life.
